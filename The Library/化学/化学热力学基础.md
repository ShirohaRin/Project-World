## 本节公式：
$\sum v_b \cdot B = 0$
$\xi = \frac{\Delta n_a}{v_a} = \frac{\Delta n_b}{v_b} = \frac{\Delta n_c}{v_c} = \frac{\Delta n_d}{v_d}$
$PV=nRT$
$U = W_k + W_p + W_f$
$\Delta U = Q + W$
恒容反应热$\Delta U = Q_V$
恒压反应热$\Delta U = Q_P-P\Delta V$
$H = U + PV$
$\Delta H = H_{end} - H_{begin}$
$Q_P = \Delta H$
$\Delta_rS^\ominus_m = \sum v_B \cdot S^\ominus_m$
$\Delta_rG_m = \Delta_rH_m - T\Delta_rS_m$
$T_c = \frac{\Delta H}{\Delta S}$
$\Delta_rG^\ominus_m = \sum_B \cdot v_B \cdot \Delta_fG^\ominus_m(B) = \Delta_rH^\ominus_m - T\Delta_rS^\ominus_m$
$\Delta_rG_m = \Delta_rG^\ominus_m + RT\ln Q$
